# Fieldwork

Seagoing observations are the foundation of my work. I've spent more than 180 days at sea, much of it on **GO-SHIP**, the international program that repeats full-depth hydrographic sections across every ocean basin about once a decade to track long-term changes in ocean heat, carbon, oxygen, and nutrients.

## A16S (2026): South Atlantic

**R/V *Roger Revelle*** · Punta Arenas, Chile → Cabo Verde · March–May 2026

The 2026 A16S expedition crossed the South Atlantic from the tip of South America to Cabo Verde during 53 days at sea. The team occupied 96 full-depth CTD-rosette stations, sampled the ocean from the surface to as deep as 6,000 m, collected more than 20,000 seawater samples, and deployed 34 Argo floats. This was the fourth GO-SHIP occupation of the A16 line since the 1990s. Together with the A16N section completed in 2023, it gives a complete cross-section of the Atlantic Ocean. I sailed on A16S as **Co-Chief Scientist**.
